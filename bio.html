I am an Associate Professor and Canada Research Chair in Sustainable Enterprise (Tier 2) at the DeGroote School of Business, McMaster University. I also serve as a Non-Resident Fellow at the Brookings Institution in Washington, DC, and an Adjunct Associate Professor at the University of Cape Town in South Africa. At DeGroote, I teach Sustainability and International Business courses within the commerce program.

My research focuses broadly on business and society, specifically examining critical social and environmental issues. My work has been published in leading academic journals, including the Journal of Management Studies, Journal of International Business Studies, Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice, and Research Policy.

If you have questions or inquiries, please reach out to me at lashitea@mcmaster.ca.
